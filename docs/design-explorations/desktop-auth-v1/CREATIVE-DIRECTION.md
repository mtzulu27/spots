# Spots: align desktop access with the existing brand

Status: exploration, not approved or implemented. No local app or hosting changes.
Creative Production board: 03ce41f4-1062-44df-9787-246a3f8f59aa.

## Source contract

- output/spots-brand-manual/STYLE_GUIDE.md: evidence-led identity, photo-first
  experience, paired editorial colors, offset backing plates, restrained outline type.
- output/spots-instagram-campaign-20261012/final/DESIGN.md: earlier campaign
  relationships; not a license to copy campaign scale into functional controls.
- apps/mobile/components/DESIGN.md and current desktop access: functional UI
  remains Montserrat, restrained gray/white surfaces and 16px controls.
- Original auth_welcome_bg.png and original logo assets are the visual anchors.

## What went wrong in the rejected exploration

Invented routes, tickets, generic buildings and isolated decorative objects did
not make the access screen feel like Spots. Historical use of a resource does not
make a newly invented replacement approved. Those nine studies are rejected.

## Hypothesis for review

Energy belongs to the photographed experience and its composition. Access controls
remain quiet and consistent with mobile. Use one editorial gesture per proposal;
never the whole brand repertoire in a single login screen. Maintain the centered
access flow: Google, separator, continue with email, terms. Do not add password
fields, registration tabs, claims, slogans or new actions to the initial screen.

## Four visual hypotheses

1. Noche compartida: recognizable warm human photo fills the canvas; a clear
   central surface protects the same access controls.
2. Encuadre editorial: one peripheral photo with a restrained pink offset plate,
   a small petrol counterpoint and a clear central functional axis.
3. Color y experiencia: light central field with cream/coral at the edges and
   one contextual photo, without illustration or decorative symbols.
4. Firma en contorno: one peripheral photograph plus a quiet outline word as
   environmental typography, original small mark and unchanged form.

These are AI-assisted static direction previews, not responsive implementations,
final masters, approved assets or evidence of real account authentication.
Do not treat generated logo/text fidelity as a replacement for original assets.

## Selection criteria

- Recognizable as Spots, not just an attractive login screen.
- One visual focus; no decorative element competes with the primary heading.
- Photo preserves a human gesture rather than becoming an arbitrary crop.
- Desktop direction still feels related to the current mobile experience.
- Choose a direction before refining or implementing it in MagicPath.

## Review result

Four previews were generated with the built-in image generator and entered into
the same Creative Production board. All are static AI-assisted previews, not
production assets. Directions 02 and 03 shifted the form away from the requested
whole-canvas center. Direction 04 enlarged the photo beyond the intended extent.
Direction 01 extended the photographic scene. These deviations are disclosed;
the previews compare art direction rather than certify geometry or source pixels.
The coordinator recommends exploring 02 next, subject to the user's selection.
